Escape

Escape is our roguelike indie game where the player is challenged to navigate through challenging procedurally generated levels to eventually fight the final boss. They must go through various levels, finding keys, healthpacks and uncovering the secrets of the world. On they way they will face numerous challenges and enemies. Outrun the clock and survive by ESCAPING!
